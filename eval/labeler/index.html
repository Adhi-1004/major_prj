Instructions and shortcuts

Watch each line and decide whether the person saying it is visibly speaking it. You never see the system's answer — decide only from the video.

  • On 1 — the speaker of the line is on screen and their mouth is producing it.
  • Mixed 2 — on screen for part of the line (e.g. a cut mid-sentence).
  • Off 3 — the line is heard but the speaker is not visibly saying it (voice-over, narration, off-camera, someone else's face, back to camera).
  • Unclear 4 — you cannot decide. Not speech 5 — not a real line.
  • Lines marked “onset requested”: if On or Mixed, step frame by frame (←/→, Shift for ±10) to the first frame where the speaker is visibly saying this line, press O, and check the query text matches what is said.
